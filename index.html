Overrides (CLI flags)

      

        Native flags: --select --ignore --extend-select --fixable --unfixable
        --extend-fixable --target-version --preview --fix --unsafe-fixes. Anything else
        Ruff supports — e.g. --line-length isn't a real flag at all, only
        --config "line-length=100" is — goes through
        --config "<path>=<toml-value>", repeatable. Real Ruff flags with
        no meaning here (no filesystem: --watch, --output-file, …) are
        accepted and simply ignored — see the notice below if any are in play.
